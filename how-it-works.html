A clear process, from first call to handover.

Six stages, explained in plain English. Timings vary from business to business, but you’ll always know where things stand.

  1. 01

    An introductory conversation

    A relaxed call or coffee to talk about your business, your plans and what you want from a sale. We’ll tell you honestly whether we think it’s a fit.

    What we’ll need from you
    Nothing. Just some time to talk.
    What you’ll get
    A clear sense of whether it’s worth going further.
    Typical timing: Week 1
  2. 02

    Confidentiality and first look

    We sign a non-disclosure agreement, then you share some headline information so we can understand how the business performs.

    What we’ll need from you
    Recent accounts, management figures and an outline of the team and customers.
    What you’ll get
    Our early view on value and structure.
    Typical timing: Weeks 1 to 3
  3. 03

    Meeting you and the business

    We spend time with you, visit the business and learn how it really works. Staff don’t need to know who we are at this stage.

    What we’ll need from you
    A few hours for a visit and a deeper conversation.
    What you’ll get
    An understanding of what you want your role to be after the sale.
    Typical timing: Weeks 2 to 5
  4. 04

    Our offer

    We set out an indicative offer in writing, often called heads of terms: the price, how it’s paid, the timetable and your role after completion.

    What we’ll need from you
    Time to review it with your accountant and solicitor.
    What you’ll get
    A clear, written offer with no hidden conditions.
    Typical timing: Weeks 4 to 8
  5. 05

    Due diligence and legal work

    We check the financial, legal and commercial detail, while solicitors on both sides draft the sale agreement. We do the heavy lifting and keep you updated.

    What we’ll need from you
    Answers to our questions and access to documents, usually via a secure online folder.
    What you’ll get
    Regular updates and early warning of anything that needs attention.
    Typical timing: Two to four months
  6. 06

    Completion and handover

    The sale completes and funds are transferred. We then carry out the handover plan we agreed together, including how and when the team and customers are told.

    What we’ll need from you
    Your time during the agreed transition period.
    What you’ll get
    A business in safe hands and a clean, well-planned exit.
    Typical timing: Agreed with you

Timings are typical for businesses of this size and will vary depending on the business and how quickly information can be gathered.

Confidentiality isn’t a stage. It runs through all of them.

  • We sign a non-disclosure agreement before you share any financial information.
  • Your business is never advertised or shown to other buyers.
  • We never contact your staff, customers or suppliers without your agreement.
  • Documents are shared through a secure, access-controlled online folder.
  • You decide when and how people are told, and we plan those conversations together.

Getting ready to sell

You don’t need any of this to start a conversation, but these steps tend to make a sale quicker and smoother.

Tidy up the figures

Up-to-date accounts, and ideally monthly management figures, make it easier to show what the business really earns.

Write down how things work

Key processes, supplier terms and customer contracts, recorded somewhere other than your head.

Lean on your team

Letting trusted staff take on more responsibility now makes the business more valuable and the handover easier.

Choose your advisers early

An accountant and solicitor with business sale experience will save you time and protect your interests.
