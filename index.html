You built something worth keeping. So do we.

We’re not a broker, and we’re not a fund working to a five-year exit. We buy established local businesses to own and run them for the long term.

That means keeping the name your customers know, the team who made it work and the relationships you’ve spent years building. You get a fair price, a clear process and a buyer you speak to directly, from the first call through to handover.

What we do once the business is ours

Buying the business is the start, not the finish. These four commitments guide every acquisition we make.

Acquire

We acquire established, profitable small businesses and shape each deal around what the owner needs.

Partner

We work alongside owners for a smooth transition, at a pace that suits them and their team.

Grow

We invest in people, processes and performance so the business keeps getting stronger.

Preserve

We protect your legacy, look after your employees and keep supporting the local community.

From first call to handover

Selling a business can feel daunting, especially if it’s the first time. We keep the process simple, explain every step and move at a pace that works for you.

See the full process

  1. 01

    An informal first conversation

    Confidential, no obligation and no paperwork. We learn about you and the business.

  2. 02

    We get to know the business

    We sign a non-disclosure agreement, then review the figures and meet you properly.

  3. 03

    You receive a clear offer

    Price, structure, timings and your role after the sale, set out in plain English.

  4. 04

    Due diligence and completion

    We work with your advisers to get to completion, then plan the handover together.
