A local buyer, building for the long term.

Trio Capital Group Ltd acquires and grows profitable small businesses across Hampshire and the surrounding counties, and keeps them thriving long after the sale.

Strategic acquisitions. Sustainable growth.

Across the South of England, many good small businesses are owned by people approaching retirement, often with no family member or obvious buyer to take over.

Too many of those businesses close, or are sold to buyers who strip them back. We think there’s a better route: a local owner who buys well-run businesses outright, keeps what makes them work and invests to help them grow.

That’s why Trio Capital Group exists. We give owners a straightforward, confidential way to sell, and give their businesses a secure long-term future.

Our mission and vision

Our mission

To give owners of good small businesses a trusted, straightforward route to sale, and to keep those businesses thriving after they do.

Our vision

A group of well-run local businesses across the South of England, each keeping its own identity, backed by shared investment, expertise and support.

How we work

Our four commitments apply to every business we buy, and every owner we speak to.

Acquire

We buy established small businesses with integrity, offering fair value and clear terms.

Partner

We work with business owners for a smooth transition, respecting everything they’ve built.

Grow

We invest in people, processes and performance, bringing the benefits of a wider group.

Preserve

We preserve legacy, protect employees and keep supporting local communities.

What we bring to the businesses we own

Being part of a group gives a small business advantages that are hard to access alone, without losing the character that customers value.

Investment

Capital for equipment, technology and growth that a small business might otherwise put off.

Shared expertise

Support with finance, marketing, recruitment and operations, so managers can focus on customers.

Development for your people

Training and clear progression, giving the team room to grow with the business.
