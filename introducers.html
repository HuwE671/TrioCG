For accountants, solicitors and advisers.

You’re often the first person a client talks to about selling. When a client’s business fits our criteria, we’ll give you a quick, clear answer and look after them properly.

A quick, honest answer

Share a few anonymised details and we’ll tell you promptly whether it’s worth an introduction.

Your client relationship comes first

We keep you informed at the stages you want to be involved in, and we won’t go around you.

Clear criteria

Profitable businesses with turnover up to £2m, a capable team, based in Hampshire, Surrey, West Sussex, Dorset or Berkshire.

Handled with care

Confidentiality agreements as standard, and a respectful, unhurried approach with owners.

Clients who aren’t ready to sell can still benefit: our business support and Sale-Ready Review help them get organised first.
