Build a business worth buying.

Whether you plan to sell in a few years or never, TRIO Business Services helps you set up, organise and run your business more efficiently, to the same standard we look for when we buy.

We know what buyers look for, because we are one.

Most small businesses aren’t hard to sell because they’re bad businesses. They’re hard to sell because too much depends on the owner, the processes live in people’s heads and the systems never quite fitted.

Those same things make a business harder to run day to day. Fixing them gives you time back now and a stronger, more valuable business later, whether you sell to us, to someone else or never sell at all.

Where to start

The Sale‑Ready Review

We look at your business through a buyer’s eyes, across the five areas that most affect how easy it is to run and how much it’s worth. You get a clear, prioritised picture of what’s working and what to tackle first.

It starts with a free, no-obligation conversation. You decide what, if anything, happens next.

Ask about a Sale-Ready Review

  1. 01

    Financial records and reporting

    Are your accounts, invoicing and management figures clear enough for a buyer to trust?

  2. 02

    Reliance on you

    How much of the business runs through you, and what would happen if you stepped back?

  3. 03

    Processes and procedures

    Is the way you work written down, or does it live in people’s heads?

  4. 04

    Do your tools fit how you work, or are they patched together with spreadsheets?

    Are accreditations, health and safety and contracts up to date and easy to find?

Support, with no strings attached

Being a buyer as well as a support partner is what gives us our perspective. Here’s how we keep the two fair.

No obligation to sell

Using our services never commits you to selling your business, to us or anyone else.

Your information stays confidential

What you share is used only to deliver the work you’ve asked for. We’ll never assess your business for acquisition unless you ask us to.

Your choice, always

If you do decide to sell one day, you’re free to sell to whoever offers the best outcome for you.

Common questions

Do I have to be thinking about selling?

No. Many businesses use our support simply to run more smoothly. Getting organised makes a business easier to run and more valuable, whatever you decide to do with it.

How do the two hourly rates work?

Everyday administrative tasks are charged at £25 per hour. Work that involves business structure, systems, processes or operational decisions is charged at £35 per hour. If a task grows from one into the other, we’ll talk to you before the rate changes wherever possible.

Can you help on a regular basis?

Yes. Support can be ad-hoc, hourly or on a regular schedule, so you get an extra pair of hands without the cost of employing another full-time member of staff.

How are business systems priced?

Business systems are built from proven templates, configured to how your business works, and run for one flat monthly fee that includes hosting and support. We map what you need first, so you know the price before anything is built.

If I use your services, will you use what you learn to buy my business cheaply?

No. Information you share with us is used only to deliver the work you’ve asked for. We’ll never assess your business for acquisition unless you ask us to, and you’re always free to sell to whoever you choose.
