The businesses we’re looking for.

Established, profitable and well run. If that sounds like your business, read on, or take our two-minute fit check.

Size

Micro and small businesses with annual turnover of up to £2m. If you’re a little over, we’d still like to hear from you.

Profitability

Consistent profit and healthy cash flow over at least the last three years. We’re buying a working business, not a turnaround project.

People

A capable team who handle much of the day-to-day. The less the business depends on one person, the smoother the handover.

Track record

An established business with a loyal customer base, a good local reputation and a proven history of trading.

Sector

B2B or B2C, in any sector. We’re particularly interested in service businesses with recurring or repeat customers, from trades and maintenance to professional and specialist services.

Owner goals

Owners planning retirement, looking for succession, or ready to hand over to someone who will look after what they’ve built.

Where we buy

We’re a local buyer. Being close by means we can spend proper time in the businesses we own and support the communities they serve.

Probably not a fit

We’d rather be upfront about this so nobody’s time is wasted. If you’re unsure, ask anyway.

  • Start-ups and businesses that haven’t yet made a profit
  • Loss-making businesses in need of a turnaround
  • Property or asset-only sales with no trading business attached
  • Businesses based well outside the South of England

Is your business a fit?

Answer five quick questions for an instant, honest indication. It takes about two minutes and nothing is sent to us unless you choose to continue.

Question 1 of 5What is your business’s annual turnover?

A rough figure from your latest accounts is fine.

Question 2 of 5Has the business made a profit in each of the last three years?

We mean profit before your own salary and any one-off costs.

Question 3 of 5Who runs the business day to day?

Be honest. This helps us understand how a handover would work.

Question 4 of 5Where is the business based?

We focus on the South of England.

Question 5 of 5When might you consider selling?

There’s no wrong answer here.
